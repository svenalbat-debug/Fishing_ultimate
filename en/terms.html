Terms of use and rules

Scope

These rules apply to all content you share with other users in Fishing Ultimate: username, spot names, notes, and messages attached to check-ins as well as short messages to friends. They apply in addition to Apple’s terms of use (Standard EULA) where you download the app from the App Store.

This is not allowed

  • Insults, threats, harassment, hate speech and discrimination
  • Illegal, pornographic or violence-glorifying content
  • Advertising, spam and fraud
  • Other people's personal data without their consent
  • Spots where fishing is prohibited, in protected areas or on private property without the owner's permission
  • Calls to violate fishing or animal welfare law

Reporting and blocking

You can report users and shared spots in the app and block users. Blocked users no longer see your spots and check-ins, and you no longer see theirs. We review reports and usually respond within 24 hours.

Consequences of violations

Content that violates these rules will be removed. Anyone who violates them repeatedly or seriously will be banned. In the case of criminal offenses we may notify the authorities.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for what you share. Only share exact positions with people you trust. Public spots only show an approximate position. By sharing, you confirm that you hold the rights to the content.
