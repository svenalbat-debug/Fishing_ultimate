Support

Fishing Ultimate is a fishing diary for the iPhone: log your catches, save spots on the map, check the weather and bite times, manage your licenses and look things up in the fish encyclopedia.

Reporting rule errors

The information on closed seasons, minimum sizes and other rules comes from the official sources named in the app and is provided without any guarantee of accuracy. If you have spotted an error or a change, please write to me with a link to the official source.

Frequently asked questions

The app can’t find my location.

On your iPhone, open Settings, Privacy & Security, Location Services. Location Services must be turned on, and “While Using the App” must be selected for Fishing Ultimate. Alternatively, you can choose a saved spot for the weather.

How do I delete my data?

Without an account, all entries are stored only on your device. You delete them one by one in the app, or all at once by removing the app from your iPhone. With an account, you delete everything under Account, Delete account and all data. This also removes the data on the server.

Do the maps work without a connection?

The standard maps load their data from the internet. For navigation without a connection, you can download an area for offline use in the Apple Maps app (iOS 17 and later).

Where are my licenses?

Under Rules & Law, Licenses & permits. There you can also set reminders before the expiry date. Whether digital copies are accepted is regulated differently by the states and water managers. If in doubt, carry the originals.
