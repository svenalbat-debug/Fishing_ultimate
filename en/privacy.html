Privacy policy

For the Fishing Ultimate app and this website. As of: November 2026

1. In brief

This app is a fishing diary. You can use it without an account. In that case all data stays on your device and is not transmitted to the provider. If you want to back up your entries and use them on several devices, you can voluntarily create an account with an email address and password. Your data is then stored on a server in the EU and synchronized between your devices. There is no advertising, no tracking and no analytics services, and the provider does not use your data for any other purposes. Details are in section 3 and section 5.

Some functions retrieve data from third-party services, for example weather data or maps. In doing so, technically necessary data such as your IP address and a location are transmitted. The details are in section 5.

3. Account, data and synchronization

The app stores on your device: catches (species, length, weight, bait, water, date, photos, weather and water temperature, rod and rig, catch location), spots (name, notes, coordinates, photos, parking, category), licenses and permits (title, issuer, number, validity, photos or PDF files), water rules, packing lists (lists and entries), reminders (title, date, notes), water maps (images or PDF files that you add yourself) and your settings.

Account and synchronization (voluntary): Only if you create an account does the app collect your email address and password. The password is not stored in plain text but only in protected form (hash). After sign-in, the app synchronizes the data listed above, including photos and files, with the server so that it is available on all devices with the same account. This also includes the coordinates you save in spots and catches. On the server the data is assigned to your account; other users cannot view it. Without an account, no data is transmitted to the server. The purpose is to provide backup and synchronization. The legal basis is performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Friends, shared spots and check-ins (voluntary): With an account you can create a profile with a username, add friends, share spots and show others with a check-in that you are at the water. For this we store your username, friendships and requests, block lists, the shared spots (name, notes, position; with the visibility “All” the position is rounded to about one kilometer), check-ins and short messages (spot name and position only for check-ins, message text, time and the selection of recipients; they are visible only to the friends you selected for four hours and are deleted afterwards) and replies to them. These contents are seen by the users you release them to. Anyone who knows your username can send you a friend request. For notifications we store the notification token of your device and the language selected in the app, so that notifications arrive in your language. Notifications are delivered through the Apple Push Notification Service. Apple processes the token and the content of the notification, for example username and spot name. We store reports from other users about content or persons in order to review violations. The legal basis is performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), for reports our legitimate interest in protecting users (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). When you delete your account, this data is deleted.

You delete individual entries in the app, or your entire account under Account, Delete account and all data. This removes all data on the server and on the device. On your device, data may also be contained in backups, for example in an iCloud or computer backup. You control this in the iOS settings.

4. Permissions

Location: The app asks for your location while you are using the app. It is used to show the location point on the map, to retrieve weather and bite times for your place, to suggest spots near you and to save the catch location. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) via the iOS prompt. You can withdraw it at any time under Settings, Privacy & Security, Location Services. Without location, you can choose saved spots instead.

Photos and files: You choose photos through the iOS photo picker. The app receives only the images you choose, not access to your whole library. The same applies to files (PDF or image).

Notifications: Reminders about expiring licenses are local notifications triggered by your device itself. There is no push server for them, and no data is transmitted.

5. Third-party services

Supabase (account, server and storage)

This applies only if you create an account. Account, sign-in, database and file storage then run on Supabase (Supabase, Inc., USA) in a data center region in the EU. Supabase processes your data on behalf of the provider on the basis of a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). For technical reasons, Supabase receives your IP address. Where data reaches third countries, this is based on the EU Commission’s standard contractual clauses. The emails with confirmation and reset codes are sent through an email service of the provider.

Open-Meteo (weather data)

For weather, air pressure, bite times and the automatic catch conditions, the app retrieves data from Open-Meteo (OpenMeteo GmbH, Hintere Schilligmatte 6, 6463 Bürglen, Switzerland). What is transmitted is the coordinates of the chosen place, rounded to about one kilometer, for a catch date the period concerned, and, for technical reasons, your IP address. According to Open-Meteo, server logs may contain IP addresses and coordinates and are deleted after 90 days. For Switzerland there is an adequacy decision by the EU Commission. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, because the request is necessary for the function you use. The location data is additionally based on your consent.

Apple Maps

With the Standard and Satellite map styles, the app loads map data through Apple’s mapping service. Apple receives technically necessary data such as your IP address and the map area shown. Apple’s privacy policy applies. If you tap links such as Navigate to parking or Open in Maps, the app passes the coordinates to the Maps app.

Own map source (optional)

Only if you enter a map address yourself under Offline maps does the app load map tiles from that provider. It receives your IP address, the tile coordinates and thus the map area, an identifier of the app and the time. The privacy terms of that provider apply.

News

For the News, the app retrieves a file with news items (news.json) from this website when it starts, when you return to the app (at most about every 30 minutes), when you open and refresh the News, and about every 15 minutes while the News are open. For technical reasons, your IP address is transmitted to the host of the website (see section 11). Your location and personal details are not sent. The app calculates closed-season notices and notices about your licenses itself on your device.

Websites

Links to official sources and other pages open in the browser. There the privacy policies of the respective operators apply.

6. Feedback to the provider

In the Feedback area the app prepares an email to the provider. You see the text before sending and send it yourself through your email app. The provider receives your message, your email address and the sender data of your email app. If you leave the option enabled, the message also contains technical details: app version, iOS version and device type. You can optionally attach a screenshot. The email goes through your email account and the provider of your email service. The purpose is to process your message and to improve the app. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, the legitimate interest in answering and in improving the app. The message is deleted as soon as it has been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

7. Download via the App Store

If you download the app from the Apple App Store, Apple processes data in the process, for example about your Apple ID and the download. Apple alone is responsible for this. The provider receives no personal data from it.

8. No advertising, no tracking

The app contains no advertising, no tracking or analytics components and no social network services. No cookies are set, and your data is neither sold nor passed on for advertising purposes.

Note: If you have allowed iOS to share analytics data with developers, Apple can provide the provider with anonymized crash reports. You control this under Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

9. Retention period

Your account and your data remain stored until you delete your account. When you delete it, the data on the server is removed without delay. Backups of the hosting provider may still contain it for a short time until they are overwritten in the regular cycle. For third-party services, their retention periods apply (see section 5). Emails with feedback are deleted as soon as they have been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

10. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). You can withdraw any consent you have given at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)).

You can view and change your data in the app and delete your account together with all data yourself. You can receive a copy of your data (Art. 15 and 20 GDPR) as well as information and deletion for emails you have sent to the provider by email to the address in section 2. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the data protection authority at your place of residence.
